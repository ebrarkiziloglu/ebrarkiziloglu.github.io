TabiBERT

A modern encoder-only model for Turkish, built on the ModernBERT architecture and pre-trained from scratch on 1T tokens. Built by Tabilab at Boğaziçi University.

Use cases

Turkish NLP

Classification, question answering, NLI and other fine-tuned tasks.

Search & retrieval

Semantic search and information retrieval over Turkish documents.

Long documents

Document classification and retrieval with up to 8,192 tokens of context.

Turkish–English

Text understanding across both languages (about 13% of the data is non-Turkish).

Code retrieval

Representation learning for code, thanks to code and English commentary in the mix.

Math & symbols

Mathematical text and symbolic content, also seen in pre-training.

Try it

from transformers import pipeline

pipe = pipeline("fill-mask", model="boun-tabilab/TabiBERT")
print(pipe("[MASK], Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'nin başkentidir.")[0]["sequence"])
# Ankara, Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'nin başkentidir.

How it came together

  • DataBuilt an 84.88B-token Turkish pre-training corpus from multiple sources.
  • ModelPre-trained a ModernBERT-based encoder for 1T tokens.
  • BenchmarkDeveloped TabiBench and compared TabiBERT against the major Turkish BERT models.
  • TalkPresented it at LatentShift 2026, İstanbul.

Paper: M. Türker, A. E. Kızıloğlu, O. Güngör, S. Üsküdarlı. TabiBERT: A Large-Scale ModernBERT Foundation Model and a Unified Benchmark for Turkish Language. arXiv:2512.23065. Under review at the Cambridge NLP Journal.
