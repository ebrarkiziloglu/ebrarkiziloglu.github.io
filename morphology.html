Does Turkish case morphology help German case sensitivity?

TUM · Data Analytics & Statistics Lab · Nov 2025 –

Two-phase pretraining of character-level LMs: Turkish (or English as control), then German. Tested on German Accusative↔Dative minimal pairs from Universal Dependencies with perplexity-based probing.

  • Zemberek
  • SMOR
  • ablations
  • TOST testing
Findings

Phase-1 pretraining gives about +4 points of German case accuracy, with no significant advantage for Turkish over English. Embedding resets hurt English→German transfer disproportionately. With M. Di Marco, L. Bylinina and L. Edman. Paper in preparation.
