Can LMs inflect Turkish correctly?

Co-supervising a bachelor thesis · Boğaziçi · Sep 2026 –
  • GitHub

Studying how well language models generate correctly inflected Turkish words, given the root and the following grammatical features, while obeying the suffix choice and order, vowel harmony and consonant alternations. Students are building a benchmark of generation tasks, a reproducible evaluation, and a taxonomy of errors. It’s the playground below, pointed at LLMs.
