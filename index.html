01 Agglutination playground

Turkish glues suffixes onto roots, and every suffix changes shape to match what is in front of it. A model has to learn those rules from characters alone. Pick a root and stack suffixes, and the rules show up below, the way a morphological analyzer such as Zemberek would see them.

    02 Research

    Does Turkish case morphology help German case sensitivity?

    TUM · Data Analytics & Statistics Lab · Prof. Alexander Fraser · Nov 2025 –

    A controlled two-phase pretraining study with GPT-2-style character-level LMs. Train on Turkish (or English as the control), then on German, and ask whether knowledge of case marking carries over. Evaluation uses German Accusative↔Dative minimal pairs built from Universal Dependencies, scored by perplexity-based probing across seeds.

    • Zemberek
    • SMOR
    • NLLB subsampling
    • ablations
    • TOST equivalence testing
    What did I find?

    Phase-1 pretraining gives about a +4 point gain in German case accuracy, but Turkish has no significant advantage over English (TOST equivalence). Ablations (word shuffling, stripping Turkish case suffixes, resetting embeddings) show that resetting embeddings hurts English→German transfer disproportionately. So some of what transfers is not about case at all. Paper in preparation with M. Di Marco, L. Bylinina and L. Edman.

    TabiBERT & TabiBench

    Bogazici University · Tabilab · Jan 2025 – Jan 2026

    A modern Turkish encoder, built end to end: an 84.88B-token pretraining corpus, a ModernBERT-based model trained for 1T tokens, and TabiBench, a unified Turkish NLP benchmark. TabiBERT sets a new state of the art on 5 of 8 tasks against the major Turkish BERTs.

    Can LMs inflect Turkish correctly?

    Co-supervisor, bachelor thesis · Bogazici University · Sep 2026 –

    Co-supervising (with Assist. Prof. Suzan Üsküdarlı) a project on root + grammatical features → inflected form: suffix choice and order, vowel harmony, consonant alternation. The students are building a benchmark of generation tasks, a reproducible evaluation program, and a taxonomy of generation errors. It is the playground above, turned around and pointed at LLMs.
